Access Rights

Who may use which app on the shared Supabase project.

Invite someone new

They get an email inviting them to choose a password, and can then sign in to exactly these apps and nothing else. Admin of this page is granted below, once the account exists.

Changes take effect the next time that person loads the app — no deploy needed.

Inviting and deleting go through invite_app_user and delete_app_user, which hold the secret key in the database where the browser cannot reach it. This page still carries only the publishable key.

Deleting is permanent and is refused for administrators and for yourself. To lock someone out while keeping their data, set every app to None — that is reversible.
